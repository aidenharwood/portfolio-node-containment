# Phase 4: people, quests and items for the Low Ward

Part of `2026-11-13-world-rebuild-design.md`. This draft is for the owner to approve before it is built, and it has been revised after comparing the first draft with how RuneScape does the same things. Every name is a placeholder the owner can replace.

## How RuneScape does it, and what changed

| | RuneScape | First draft | Now |
| - | --------- | ----------- | --- |
| **Tutorial** | Tutorial Island: short and self-contained, one instructor per system, then you are let loose | Ran across half the city and straight into the main story | A short tutorial in Terminus Square, one instructor per system, ending with "the city's yours" |
| **Main story** | None at the start. You find quests by talking to people; quest series and lore come later | A four-quest spine that pulled the player down the Site | The Site's mystery is a **quest series** you find for yourself, gated by skills and earlier quests |
| **First quests** | Novice quests that each teach a skill with a small, often funny problem (Cook's Assistant, Sheep Shearer, Restless Ghost), plus one early boss quest (Demon Slayer) | Side quests were extras | Seven **novice quests** from the residents, one per skill, plus an early boss quest |
| **Gear** | Material ladders that are the same everywhere (bronze, iron, steel, mithril, adamant, rune), each a full set, gated by level | Four sets named after places | **Material ladders** with a full set per tier, gated at levels 1, 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 |
| **Resources** | Each skill has a level ladder of spots (normal, oak, willow, maple, yew trees) spread over the map | One kind of node per skill | A **level ladder of spots** per gathering skill, placed where the story puts them |
| **Monsters** | Combat levels, passive critters near the start that drop useful things, aggression only towards low-level players, drop tables with a rare drop | Two creatures per area by tier | **Levelled creatures** from passive pests up, each with a drop table and a rare drop |
| **Rewards** | Experience, quest points, and above all **access**: new areas, shortcuts, items you can't buy, the right to wear something | Clearances and items | Quests unlock lifts, shortcuts, shop stock and gear; **quest points** gate the region's end |

## Last Train In: the tutorial

It stays inside Terminus Square and its station, like an island. It runs in a fixed order, and each instructor teaches one thing by having you do it. It takes about ten minutes.

| Instructor | Where | You learn |
| ---------- | ----- | --------- |
| Wren Adebayo | Station platform | Moving, talking, examining things, the journal |
| Moss Kettering | General store | Buying and selling, scrip |
| The locker clerk | Locker office | Storage and the exchange post |
| Officer Halloran | Response post and the rail yard | Equipping gear and fighting a training drone with each style. Then subduing and containing a harmless anomaly in a crate |
| Li Vance | Noodle bar | Eating, and why food matters |
| Sergeant Doyle (comes to the square) | By the clock tower | Contracts, the map, and where things are |

At the end Doyle says the city is yours, and the rest of the Ward opens. Nothing more is required of you.

## Novice quests

There is one per skill line, each with a funny or odd little problem, all in the home squares. Each one needs nothing to start, and each rewards experience in the skill it teaches, a quest point and something useful.

| Quest | Given by | Skill | The problem | Unlocks |
| ----- | -------- | ----- | ----------- | ------- |
| **Noodle Run** | Li Vance | Salvage, biochemistry | The Ward's best broth needs an eel from the basin, greens from the allotments and day-old bread from Ostrowski's | Vance's specials in his shop |
| **Line Thief** | Ada Okafor | Containment | Something wearing her husband's coat is living in the courtyard | Ada's coat, a cosmetic |
| **Bed Six** | Agnes Pole | Biochemistry | The plant with leaves like hands has grown back, and it waves | Agnes lets you use the allotments |
| **Valve Trouble** | Kofi Mensah | Engineering, refining | Three sluice valves in the Cut have seized; make the parts and fit them | A culvert shortcut from the Cut to the basin |
| **Rent Day** | Bettina Crane | Research | A tenant hasn't paid in months and nobody has seen him. His room is full of notes about the knocking | The Site series |
| **Lost Cat** | Pell | Fortitude, close quarters | The grey cat went down the alley vent | The vent route into the culverts |
| **Off the Books** | Dutch Hallam | Marksmanship | Something on the heaps keeps stealing the good scrap at night. Watch the yard and shoot what moves | Hallam buys and sells tier-two salvage |

**The early boss quest: The Drowned Lamp** (Sol Marsh, needs Lost Cat). The lamp the pub is named after was pulled from the canal still lit, and something came with it. It sets up a small fight in the culverts, the region's first named boss, at around combat level 15. The reward is the lamp itself, which you can carry as a light source.

## The Site Meridian series

The mystery of the Site becomes a series, as RuneScape grows its lore, and you find it by talking to people. Each quest lists its requirements in the journal.

1. **The Steel Below.** Needs Rent Day. Tam's steel, Quill's ledgers and Clerk Ferris's silence. The reward is B1 clearance: the headhouse lift runs for you.
2. **Dead Band.** Needs The Steel Below and engineering 15. Build Sefa's receiver and take readings in the Cut, the Scrapyards and the Shipyard Fringe. All three point under the Site.
3. **Bill of Lading.** Needs Dead Band and research 25. Into B2's records through the breached ward. The Vault was dug before the Site was built. The reward is B2 clearance.
4. **The Heart.** Needs Bill of Lading, 12 quest points and combat level 40. Down through the Vault to **the Warden**, the keter-class anomaly holding the shaft shut from the inside. Beyond it is a sealed hatch going further down, and the Cordon gate is ordered open, which leads to the next region.

There are more side quests at higher levels: **What Glows** (Hetty Graves), **Knock Back** (Corporal Sato, inside the beached hull) and **Bottom Up** (Kofi Mensah, the flood).

## Gear: material ladders

There are six materials, each a full set across every style and slot. The names are shared, as in RuneScape: "iron baton", "iron carbine", "iron helm". Requirements are skill levels only.

| Material | Level | Made from |
| -------- | ----- | --------- |
| Scrap | 1 | Scrap metal from the heaps, hammered flat |
| Iron | 10 | Basin dredgings refined to iron bars |
| Steel | 20 | Iron and coke from the Scrapyards' crusher |
| Alloy | 30 | Shipyard plate from the Fringe |
| Ceramite | 40 | Directorate salvage from B2 |
| Vault glass | 50 | Residue from the Vault's chambers |

- **Sets:** each material has close-quarters weapons (baton, blade, maul), marksmanship weapons (pistol, carbine, rifle), psionic weapons (focus, resonator) and armour (helm, vest, legs, gloves, boots).
- **Crafting:** engineering makes the gear at a fabricator. Refining turns raw salvage into bars at a refinery.
- **Shops:** shops sell scrap and iron. Steel and above have to be made, found or traded on the exchange.

## Gathering ladders

| Skill | Spots, by level |
| ----- | --------------- |
| Salvage | Scrap heap (1), basin dredge spot (10), wreck plate in the Fringe (20), Directorate salvage on B2 (30), Vault residue (40) |
| Biochemistry | Allotment greens (1), pond reeds (10), culvert fungus (20), hand-leaf from Bed Six (30), Vault bloom (40) |
| Research | Faint trace (1), culvert trace (10), Fringe trace (20), red-class trace on B2 (30), Vault trace (40) |

Biochemistry turns its harvest into food and medicine at the synthesiser.

## Creatures

Creatures have combat levels, not tiers. Each one has a drop table that feeds a skill and a rare drop worth talking about.

| Where | Creatures (level) | Aggressive |
| ----- | ----------------- | ---------- |
| Home squares | Canal rat (2), pigeon (1), stray dog (4) | No |
| The Cut | Gutter mite (6), silt eel (9) | Only to players below their level |
| The Scrapyards | Scrap crawler (14), rustling (20) | Yes |
| B1 | Moth-lantern (12), glass hound (22) | Yes |
| The Shipyard Fringe | Hull knocker (28), tide wraith (34) | Yes |
| B2 | Red twin (38), cell-breaker (44) | Yes |
| The Cordon | Cordon stalker (46) | Yes |
| B3 | Vault echo (52); the Warden (boss, 65) | Yes |

Containable anomalies are the moth-lantern, the rustling, the glass hound, the tide wraith, the red twin and the Warden. Containment level gates which ones you can take. Each creature gets a voxel model in the phase 2 style.

## Engine work this needs

- **Quest points:** each quest awards some, with a `questPoints` condition. This is new.
- **Map markers:** quest-start icons on the map for quests you can take, unless they already exist.
- **Lift clearance:** a `hasFlag` condition on each lift, set by the series.
- **Content files:** `content/quests/`, `content/items/` (generated per material ladder), `content/creatures/`, gathering spots and crafting stations as kit objects.
- **Balance tests:** rewritten for the ladders. Each material is craftable from spots at or below its level, kill times stay level by tier, and experience per hour climbs smoothly.

## Status

**Step 1 (built).**
- **Ladders:** the scrap ladder (level 1) and the iron ladder (level 10), each with eight weapons and fifteen armour pieces in three lines:
  - **Bulwark (fortitude):** Scrap and Iron.
  - **Skirmisher (marksmanship):** Patchwork and Oilskin.
  - **Warden (psionics):** Sackcloth and Felt.
- **Materials and food:** twelve materials and four foods and medicines, with refinery, fabricator and synthesiser recipes. Three rare drops.
- **Gathering spots:** scrap piles, faint traces, eel traps and allotment beds at level 1. Dredge winches, reed beds and culvert traces at level 10.
- **Stations:** a refinery and fabricator in Fenwick Tools and a synthesiser in Greenhalgh Remedies.
- **Shops:** stock for every shop, plus Vance's and Ostrowski's.
- **Creatures:** five creatures with their own models: training drone, crate wisp, canal rat, pigeon and stray dog.
- **Last Train In** is the starting quest, and **Noodle Run** is the first novice quest.

**Engine changes**
- **Quest points:** `questPoints` on a quest and a `questPoints` condition. Tutorials award none by default.
- **Innermost area decides safety:** a fenced yard can be hostile inside a safe square.
- **Shop stock as a source:** the crafting checks count items sold by a placed shopkeeper as available.
- **District tiers follow the level ladder:** home squares 1; the Canal Basin and the Cut 3; the Scrapyards and B1 6; the Fringe 8; the Cordon and B2 10; the Vault 12.
- **Critter yards:** the creatures for steps 1 and 2 live in fenced yards inside the home squares, the rail yard behind the station and the allotments, as RuneScape keeps chickens and cows in pens.
