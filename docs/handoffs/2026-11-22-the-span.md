# Handoff: the Span replaces Hollow Bay

Written 2026-11-22. The world and every quest were deleted and rebuilt to `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-the-span-design.md`.

## What landed

- **Generic assets moved into `content/catalog/`**: gear ladders, materials, recipes, nodes, stations, the furnishing kit, landmark props and prefabs, uniques, mastery mantles, currency and services, plus the creature and dialogue helpers.
- **The Span**: first drawn as a 16 × 6 strip, it is now (2026-11-23, see below) an 18 × 14 island with five Seams holding three gates each, Harrow's terraces, Halcyon's tower skyline, the *Meridian* hull, the glass plateau, coolant at the Spindle and the river Spill. It is cut into 138 land squares across six regions, plus three facility levels retitled from the Site Meridian layouts: `kestrel-intake`, `gallowglass-undercity` and `null-below`.
- **Content in `content/span/`**:
  - 31 creatures, five of them bosses with encounters: the Orchard Keeper, Mr Brightly, the Cohort, the Precentor and the Warden of Null.
  - 18 Span landmarks, the Seam gates, the lifts and their clearance flags.
  - 58 NPCs and 27 shops.
  - Six regions and their contracts.
- **Quests** (`span/quests/`): six acts (`intake.ts` for Act I; `spinward.ts` for Acts II–III; `farSpan.ts` for Acts IV–VI and the two-way finale, with flags `ending:resealed` and `ending:signed-out`), plus a side questline in every settlement.
- **Tests**: tests pinned to Hollow Bay were retargeted, `hinterland.test.ts` was removed, and `wardBalance.test.ts` became `spanBalance.test.ts`. Balance tuning that came out of it: the rustling flings pellets, the neon moth fires darts, and the Spindle creatures have defence 66, accuracy 72 and 1.35× experience.

## Update 2026-11-23: the Span unrolled

The overworld was rebuilt to `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-the-span-unrolled.md`. It is now an open island of 18 × 14 squares, the Reach, round the Quiet Massif, with the six regions running clockwise and the Spindle on the summit, reached only from the Glass Steppe through the Spindle Seam. Every pair of neighbouring regions meets at a Seam with three gates. Tier rises with walking distance from Site Kestrel. The land plan gained `relief`, `soften` and `origin` (see `WORLD_DESIGN.md`, rule 7a), so the map can grow in any direction by lowering `origin` and widening the plan.

**Transit now exists.** The Span Transit runs on drawn track through walled terminals at six hubs (`content/span/transit.ts`): the Ring Line (Kestrel Commons, Harrowgate, Neon Row, Rustwater, the Choir) and the Spindle Funicular (the Choir to Last Post). Kestrel Commons is known from the start, the rest are found on foot, and bridges cross the track in every square it runs through.

## How to work

- The map itself is data in `scripts/span/atlas.ts`: the `GRID` of region codes, `REGIONS` (tiers and relief), `PLACES`, `SEAMS`, `RAIL` and `WILD_PINS`. After changing it, run the three scripts in order: `node scripts/span/drawLand.ts` redraws the land plan, `node scripts/span/cutSquares.ts` regenerates **every** layout file and the layout index, and `node scripts/span/issues.ts` reports what is left. Settlement buildings, NPC placements and room recipes are in `scripts/span/places.ts` and `recipes.ts`. Quest objects placed on the ground are in `scripts/span/extras/`. `node scripts/span/issues.ts` prints the content issues grouped by kind.
- While the Span is still being shaped, change the generator and re-run it rather than editing layouts by hand. Once someone hand-edits a layout in the editor, stop re-running `cutSquares`, or it will overwrite their work.

## Open items

- **Nobody has walked it.** There was no visual check: this session was terminal-only. Walk round from Kestrel to the Spindle in a live game, ride both lines, and check the terraces, the beaches and softened slopes, Halcyon's towers, the hull, the glass plateau, the bridges over the track and the gate channels for cliffs and seams.
- **Quest objects only stand on the ground.** The casualty board, the bunk frame and the seed drawer stand outside the buildings the story puts them in. Extend `extras` to accept a building and a spot.
- **No choice between containing and killing Mr Brightly.** Every objective in a step is required, so the quest can only ask for one. A real choice needs an "any of" objective in the engine.
- **Missing facility levels.** Site Orchard, Site Tallow and Site Vesper have none, and Vesper's lift is decorative.
- **Growing the generator.** The plan supports `origin`, but `drawLand.ts` does not write one yet: everything is laid out from square 0,0 of `GRID`. Adding land to the west or north through the generator means teaching `atlas.ts` a grid origin; adding it to the east or south only needs wider `GRID` rows.
- **The Ring Line is not a ring.** It ends at the Choir and does not cross the Drop back to Kestrel, since nothing crosses the Drop. Closing it needs a bridge or a causeway over the Drop.
- **`drawLand` is slow.** One `drawLand` call costs about 1 s, dominated by `findCliffs`, `soften` and `fillPolygon`, and the generator calls it several times (the relief pass and each pocket-filling pass). This is a possible optimisation if the land grows.
- The old `scripts/_*.ts` scratch scripts still reference Hollow Bay modules. They are not typechecked.
