# Handoff: Act II pacing and guidance

Written 2026-11-17. This continues `2026-11-17-gameplay-critique-done.md` and works through its open items, using the critic-loop rules from `2026-11-17-gameplay-critique.md`.

The critic was Opus at high effort, because Fable 5.2 needed usage credits. Its verdicts: rounds 1 and 2 "not yet", round 3 **"ship it"**.

## Findings

1. **Research 25 was a guidance gap more than a wall.**
   - Bill of Lading's gate is Research 25 (37,564 XP).
   - About 40 minutes of trace study gets a player there from where Dead Band leaves them, and first studies of creatures pay 2–5k each.
   - Nothing in the game said so, and the tracker went empty after Dead Band.
2. **Crawlers were the best farm from combat 13 to 38.** Kill XP was nearly flat past level 16, and regeneration was a flat 1 integrity every 20 ticks. Both kept players on scrap crawlers.
3. **The midgame had no gear path or reward items.**
   - No shop sold anything above iron.
   - What Glows, Bottom Up and Knock Back paid small, fixed amounts of XP and no items.
   - Contracts stopped at tier 4.
4. **Smaller items.** The Okafor coat dropped from line mimics forever, and Pike's Pawn had no stock.

## What changed

**Story chain and the `reach` objective**
- New quest objective `{ type: 'reach', measure: SkillId | 'combat' | 'questPoints', level, label }`.
  - It reads player state through `QuestStateProbe.levelOf`.
  - It is re-checked on a new `standingChanged` quest event, raised on each level-up and after each quest completes.
- The chain now runs `dead-band` → `bill-of-lading` → `the-heart` through `followUpQuestId`.
  - Each chapter opens with a `ready` step of `reach` objectives: Research 25 for Bill of Lading, and combat 40 plus 12 quest points for The Heart.
  - Each `ready` step has a journal saying how to get there, and an `onComplete` notice.
  - The gated offers are gone. Their story now sits in step reports: Brandt's `brief` and Hale's `hale`.
- `questTalk` gained `notes` (per-step NPC lines). It also now shows `waiting` whenever there is text, not only when the NPC has an offer.
- `QuestProgress.stepId` is saved, and `realignedProgress` keeps saves on the right step when steps are inserted.
  - `stepsAddedBeforeStepIds: 1` on both chapters shifts older saves without a `stepId` once.

**Tracker**
- Shows "Recommended combat level N · yours M" when the player is under the quest's recommended level.
- Shows XP for skill goals, for example "11 · 8,120 / 37,564 XP".

**Pacing**
- `killExperience` is now `6L + 2·min(L,16)² + 8·(min(L,34)−14)⁺²` (`ward/novice.ts`).
- Regeneration is 5% of max integrity every 20 ticks, after the 16-tick out-of-combat delay (`shared/src/combat/regeneration.ts`).
- Counting the time spent healing, the best target now climbs:
  - glass hounds from combat 13;
  - hull knockers from about combat 20;
  - Cordon stalkers never ahead of knockers before 35.
- In the model, ideal combat time from 13 to 48 is about 53 minutes, against about 106 before.

**Contracts** (`content/contracts.ts`)
- Tier 6–8 dailies:
  - hound-watch;
  - amber-survey (pays Research);
  - heavy-haul;
  - steel-pour;
  - knock-shift;
  - plate-salvage;
  - wraith-tide.
- Weeklies: fringe-sweep and trace-ledger.
- The Contracts Office now also sells heavy scrap, amber trace, steel bar and hull plate for commendations.

**Shops** (`seed/lowWard.ts`, about 1.4× item value)
- After Dead Band:
  - Fenwick: steel baton, blade, helm and plate.
  - Lind: steel pistol and focus.
  - Abara: rigger hood and jacket.
  - Greenhalgh: vigil cowl and robe.
- After Knock Back, Pike: alloy baton, helm, plate, pistol and focus, the slipway hood and the tidewarden cowl.
- From the start, Pike also sells second-hand tier-2 extremities and two iron guns.

**Quest rewards** (`ward/depths.ts`)
- XP scales with the level step at a set level.
- What Glows: 3,000 Biochemistry, 1,500 Research and a new craftable **Survey Loupe** (eyes, Insight 4, Research 12; fabricator at Engineering 12, from 2 silt iron and 3 culvert traces).
- Bottom Up: 3,800 Engineering, 1,500 Refining and 4 steel bars.
- Knock Back: 5,400 Fortitude, 2,700 Close Quarters and an alloy pavise. Its recommended level is now 24.

**Loot**
- Quest-category loot drops only while an active quest still needs it (`activeQuestWantsItem`, used in `combatController.lootFor`).

**Tests**
- The `reach` objective, step realignment and the legacy step shift.
- Quest-item wanting.
- The full chain links.
- No gated offer on a follow-up chapter, and enough quest points before The Heart.
- Every waiting line can be heard.
- Per-minute pacing that counts healing.
- 727 tests in total.

## Open items

1. **Check the live database before deploying.** The legacy shift assumes no Act II save already has a `stepId`. The local DB has no character partway through Act II.
2. **Watch Act II in playtesting.** The model has it about twice as fast as before. If it feels short, the levers are `REGENERATION_SHARE` and the `8·(L−14)²` coefficient.
3. **Creature threat past tier 3 looks low.** In the fight model, a combat-25 operative beats L44 creatures about 97% of the time. This was not changed.
4. **Still open from the earlier handoff:** a live in-game fight capture with bloom.
5. **The HUD shows a stuck "Loading" chip** under the character in headless shots of Terminus Square. It was already there before this work and was not investigated.

## Done since (second pass)

- **Route hint for `reach` objectives.** `reach` takes an optional `toward: { objects?, creatures?, study? }`.
  - `study` creatures drop out once studied. `RouteScene.studiedCreatures` and `objectiveCreatureIds(..., studied)` carry that.
  - The minimap route, the world highlights and `objectiveDistricts` all follow the hint. `findContentIssues` checks the ids.
  - Bill of Lading's Research 25 points at culvert and amber traces, and at moth lanterns, crawlers, rustlings, glass hounds and hull knockers not yet studied. The Heart's combat 40 points at hull knockers.
  - A test requires every story-chain skill or combat gate to have a hint.
- **Lost Cat gauntlet test** (`wardBalance.test.ts`, `gauntletWinRate`). Three mites back to back, fresh from the tutorial in the issue kit (Close Quarters 4, Fortitude 3):
  - about 14% with no food;
  - about 82% eating one smoked eel between fights;
  - about 99% eating two.
  - At the recommended level, one eel between fights gives 96%.
  - The test pins "risky dry, safe with eels".
- **Tracker shot headlessly** with a preview character partway into Bill of Lading. Both new lines render. The XP counter had wrapped into a narrow column beside the label, so it now sits on its own line (`.objective-experience`).
  - `scripts/_act2state.mjs` (local, never committed) puts the preview character into that state. Back up `preview.sqlite` first, and restore it after.
