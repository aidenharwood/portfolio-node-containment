## Round 1 verdict: not yet. Applied, uncommitted, typecheck green, not yet shot

- **Chat on compact layouts:**
  - When chat is collapsed, toasts show examine, notice, error and whisper lines.
  - An unread dot appears on the chat toggle.
  - Chat only auto-scrolls when it is already at the bottom.
  - Chat tabs have tab roles, and tapping a tab opens collapsed chat.
- **Side panel:**
  - Shows the active tab's name as a heading on touch and compact layouts.
  - `aria-selected` is cleared on collapse.
- **`gameScreen.ts` `fitHudColumns`:**
  - Caps the left column (party) and the objectives list at the top of the chat column, and hides the objectives list when it would be cramped.
  - In compact landscape, moves objectives into the left column, so they no longer overlap the party invite.
  - In compact layouts, moves the activity bar into the stack above chat.
  - In portrait, shifts the camera up by half the covered height while the panel is open (`CameraRig.shiftViewUp`, using `setViewOffset`).
- **UI scale** is capped by viewport height: at most 1.1 on compact layouts and 1.5 on desktop.
- **`mobile.css`:**
  - The market window joins the shop and storage rules.
  - Touch-target minimums cover the contain button, chat actions, party leave, quest and bestiary rows, and roster delete.
  - Roster padding respects left and right safe areas.
  - Portrait column widths no longer overlap.
  - On compact layouts the loading chip is a small spinner on the minimap corner, at z-index below the panels.
  - In landscape with the panel open, the minimap buttons keep their surface.
  - Objectives hide during dialogue in landscape.
- **`hud.css`:**
  - Desktop dialogue has a max height.
  - Loading chip z-index is 11.
  - Loadout slot labels have a 10px floor, with short labels (Main, Off, Chip, Util).
